version: 0.23.0
## See what's new
KRIT now shows a What's New panel after each update, so you never miss a feature.

## Video editor
- Open **Edit recording** on a clip for a full editor: live zoom preview, a timeline with filmstrip and trim, and a zoom track you click to add and drag to resize.
- **Auto-zoom follows your cursor**, recorded while you capture. Tune zoom level, smoothness, follow speed and focus margin, or set a fixed center.
- Put the video on a **gradient or a real wallpaper** with padding and rounded corners, baked into the export.

## Capture
- The **Space preview** plays the video instead of a static thumbnail.
- The selection **magnifier** now appears only while holding **Control**, so aiming stays snappy. Toggle it in Preferences -> Selection.

## Fixes
- Closing the video editor no longer leaves a clip running in the background.
- The area-selection cursor reliably shows the crosshair target.
