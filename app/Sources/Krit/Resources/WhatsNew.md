### KRIT v0.28.0

The headline: Live Annotation. Press ⌘⇧D and draw directly on your screen — arrows, strokes, text, numbered steps — over any app, live, while you present or record.

### Live Annotation
- Freehand, highlighter, arrow, line, rectangle, ellipse, text and auto-numbered steps on a floating toolbar, with six colors and three widths
- Esc hands mouse and keyboard back to your apps while every mark stays pinned on top, click-through; ⌘⇧D again resumes the drawing right where you left it
- Your screen-share audience and KRIT's own recordings see the ink; the toolbar chrome never appears in a capture
- The camera button shoots the annotated screen straight into your history
- Presentation Zoom and Live Annotation coordinate — engaging one steps the other out of the way

### Feature Tour
- A six-slide tour of KRIT's highlights greets new installs and lives in the menu bar under "Feature Tour"
